## Audience
Developers with a day job who want to ship a side project that makes its first money.
Mostly India and Southeast Asia, early in their careers, short on time.

## Tone

- warm
- nerdy

## Words I never use
hustle, grind, game-changer, unlock, guru, 10x, synergy, crushing it
