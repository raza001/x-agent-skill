1/ A thread on pricing your first app

2/ Most indie devs price by fear. They pick the lowest number that doesn't feel embarrassing, then wonder why revenue never moves.

3/ Also, cheap.

4/ Ask your longest-paying users what they would use if your app vanished. Whatever they name, and whatever it costs, is your real competitor and your real price anchor.

5/ Raise the price for new users only. Let existing users keep their price for life. You keep their goodwill and test the new number on people with no old price in their heads.
